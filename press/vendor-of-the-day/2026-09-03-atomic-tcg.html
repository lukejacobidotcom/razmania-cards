Vendor of the Day. One table from the RazMania 2026 floor, every day, chosen from The Best of RazMania lists. Today: the Pokémon vendor with the biggest audience in the building.

The biggest following on the floor

Walk the Pokémon aisle at RazMania and most tables are run by a collector who turned a habit into a weekend business. Atomic TCG is something else: a Michigan vending operation with 28,000 people following along, built one video at a time by the man behind the table.

Atomic is Sean. He films and edits every video himself, 375 posts and counting, and the videos are the business as much as the cards are. That is why the number matters. Of the 153 tables that exhibited at RazMania 2026, nobody else came close to his audience, and it is the reason Atomic TCG sits at #1 on the Pokémon Power Tables list and #2 on Michigan's Finest.

Why the table is a fixture, not a one-off

Plenty of vendors show up for a weekend and disappear. Sean publishes his vending schedule publicly, which is the tell: when a dealer commits dates in advance, in public, the table is a business with a calendar, not a garage clear-out. Collectors who follow him know where he will be next before we do.

At RazMania he ran tables 104 and 105, both days, at the UWM Sports Complex in Pontiac. The focus is Pokémon and TCG, and the audience is the kind that arrives already knowing what is in the case, because they watched it get opened.

How the ranking works

The Best of RazMania lists are built from each exhibitor's own registration and their public Instagram, ordered by audience size as of August 2026. Nothing is placed by hand, nothing can be bought, and no exhibitor is #1 on more than one list. The full method is on the lists page, and every one of the ten lists is ten tables deep.
