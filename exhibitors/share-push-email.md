## Before you send — three things to check

1. **The reshare promise is real work.** Every exhibitor who posts gets reshared; that's
   the whole reason this email converts. Have someone on stories Wed–Fri actually doing it.
   If you can't staff it, cut that line rather than break it.
2. **Send from a person, not `info@`.** Reply-to a real inbox — the "tell us what's wrong
   and we'll fix it" line invites replies and those replies are worth having four days out.
3. **Spot-check five merged links before the blast.** One broken `{{page_url}}` in a send
   like this reads as sloppiness about *their* page, which is exactly the wrong note.
