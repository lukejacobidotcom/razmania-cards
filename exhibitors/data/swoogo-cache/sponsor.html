Why they’re on our radar

614 Rips sits at an intersection we like: sneakers, sports cards and Pokémon, sold from the same counter.

What caught our attention was not the inventory list. In July they built out a proper display case inside the shop to show slabs and singles the way a card store should, and collectors started bringing them real collections.

A collector drove in from Indiana this summer to sell them an entire vintage Pokémon collection. That is the kind of trust you do not manufacture.

They are on the show circuit most weekends, and they are buying at this one.
